# NOESIS: Master Academic Index & Curriculum Architecture
## Philosophy of Mind, Neurophilosophy, AI, Explanation & AI Ethics
### Calibrated for IIT & Central University Faculty Selections, GATE Philosophy & UGC-NET Paper II

---

### Master Pedagogical Architecture: The Four-Tier Difficulty Framework

| Tier | Title | Cognitive & Evaluative Focus | Target Thinkers / Concepts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Basic / Foundational** | Canonical definitions, classical thought experiments, foundational doctrines | Descartes, Princess Elisabeth, Hempel DN Model, Turing Imitation Game, Bostrom Orthogonality |
| **Tier 2** | **Moderate / Systematic** | Premise-conclusion deductive logic, formal counterexamples, standard objections | Block's China Brain, Salmon Causal-Mechanical, Harnad Symbol Grounding, Chouldechova Fairness Impossibility |
| **Tier 3** | **Advance / Dialectical** | Cross-paradigm tension, second-generation syntheses, edge-case failure modes | Kim Causal Exclusion, Woodward Interventionism, Humphreys Opacity, Fricker Algorithmic Injustice |
| **Tier 4** | **Conceptually Hard / Frontiers** | Faculty-interview caliber mathematical problems, boundary edge-cases | IIT Unfolding Argument, Rashomon Sets in XAI, Mechanistic Superposition, Parfitian Uploading |
