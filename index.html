The Four-Tier Pedagogical Calibration

Every problem is strictly calibrated to evaluate specific cognitive depths demanded in higher academic selections:

Tier 1: Basic
Foundational Canon

Classical definitions, canonical thought experiments, primary historical axioms (Descartes, Hempel, Turing, Bostrom).

Tier 2: Moderate
Systematic Arguments

Premise-conclusion logic, formal objections, standard counterexamples (Block, Salmon, Harnad, Chouldechova).

Tier 3: Advance
Dialectical Tensions

Cross-paradigm critique, second-generation syntheses, edge-case failure modes (Kim, Woodward, Humphreys, Fricker).

Unfolding arguments, Rashomon sets, mechanistic superposition, digital moral patiency (IIT, Olah, Couldry).
