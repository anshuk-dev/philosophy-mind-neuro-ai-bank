# NOESIS: Mind, Neurophilosophy, AI & Science Question Bank & Simulator
### High-Precision Academic Question Bank, 4-Tier Evaluative Framework, and Interactive Web App
### Calibrated for Assistant Professor Interviews (IITs & Central Universities), GATE Philosophy & UGC-NET Paper II

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview
**NOESIS** is an authentic, research-grade philosophical question bank, diagnostic assessment suite, and interactive web application designed specifically for candidates preparing for **Assistant Professor interviews and research admissions at the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and Central Universities**, as well as higher competitive examinations including **GATE Philosophy (XH-C4)** and **UGC-NET Paper II**.

The curriculum is structured around five specialized modern domains:
1. **Philosophy of Mind**: Substance dualism, physicalism, functionalism, qualia, mental causation, panpsychism, and higher-order thought.
2. **Neurophilosophy**: Co-evolutionary research ideology, neural correlates of consciousness (NCC), predictive processing, Markov blankets, and empirical volition.
3. **Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence**: The Turing test, Lovelace's objection, Chinese Room, symbol grounding, systematicity, the frame problem, and world models.
4. **Philosophy of Explanation & Explainable AI (XAI)**: Deductive-Nomological models, explanatory asymmetry, contrastive why-questions, interventionism, epistemic opacity, Rashomon sets, and mechanistic superposition.
5. **AI Ethics, Alignment & Governance**: The orthogonality thesis, instrumental convergence, mesa-optimization, fairness impossibility theorems, responsibility gaps, and artificial moral patiency.

---

## 🎯 Four-Tier Pedagogical Architecture
Every subject domain is partitioned into four distinct cognitive layers:
- **Tier 1: Basic / Foundational**: Precision in canonical definitions, primary historical axioms, and demarcation of core doctrines.
- **Tier 2: Moderate / Systematic**: Deductive premise-conclusion validity, formal counterexamples, and systematic objections.
- **Tier 3: Advance / Dialectical**: Second-generation critiques, cross-paradigm tensions, and nuanced philosophical pivots.
- **Tier 4: Conceptually Hard / Frontiers**: Advanced faculty-interview caliber problems, formal mathematical/computational models, and fine-grained boundary conditions.

---

## 🌐 Interactive Web Application Features
- **Mobile & Desktop Fully Responsive**: Built with Tailwind CSS, touch-friendly tap targets, and drawer navigation.
- **Zero Raw Markdown Asterisks**: Custom `renderRich()` parser protects KaTeX math (`$...$` and `$$...$$`) and renders all bold/italic/code tags into semantic HTML.
- **Instant 4-Tier Practice Mode**: Filter by Domain, Difficulty Tier, and Source with immediate feedback and complete philosophical groundings.
- **Timed Full-Length Mock Simulator**: Live countdown clock, official marking scheme (+2.00 / -0.66), question palette, flagged review, and instant performance diagnostics.
- **Curriculum Search Engine**: Real-time keyword filtering across questions, thinkers, and distractor analyses.
- **Revision Bookmarking Hub**: Save challenging questions to `localStorage` for quick pre-interview review.
- **Dark Mode**: Seamless toggle between academic paper reading mode and dark mode.
